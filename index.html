Head to head with the betting marketDe-vigging removes the bookmaker's built-in margin so the odds become fair probabilities. The benchmark uses proportional de-vig (normalising the inverse odds). The pipeline now also stores Shin de-vig, which accounts for the favourite-longshot bias by modelling informed money — the sharper method — and the market benchmark switches to it on the next odds refresh.

On the 210 matches of 2025/26 with Pinnacle closing odds (de-vigged), this model scored RPS 0.2068 vs the market's 0.1994 — about 0.007 behind the sharpest public forecast in existence, from goals and xG alone. Closing odds only exist at kickoff; this model forecasts the whole season.

Calibration — do the probabilities mean what they say?A forecast is calibrated if things it calls 30% likely happen about 30% of the time. Each point bins the walk-forward predictions and plots the average predicted probability against how often it actually happened; the closer to the diagonal, the better. ECE (Expected Calibration Error) is the average gap — lower is better.

Reliability of the model's win/draw/loss probabilities on the held-out backtest — a different, complementary check to RPS: RPS rewards sharpness, this asks whether the probabilities are honest.

Live track record — 2026/27, scored as it happens

Every Monday the model publishes forecasts for the upcoming fixtures; the following week they are scored against what actually happened (RPS and Brier, same metrics as the backtest). No retro-fitting — each forecast is frozen before kickoff.

What was tested and rejected

A promoted-club correction (attack ×0.59, defence ×1.57 measured over 28 promoted seasons) — rejected because the multi-season joint fit already prices promotion in. Long decay half-lives — rejected, 250 days won the sweep. Pure-xG fitting — rejected, a 0.4 blend beats it. Nothing ships here without beating the backtest first.

Attack = scoring multiplier vs an average side. Defence = conceding multiplier (lower is better). Fitted ratings come from a joint Poisson fit of 2,796 Premier League + Championship results, 2023–26.

Open data & API
Every number here is served as static JSON, CORS-open so you can build on it — no key, no rate limit.
  • /model.json — the full model bundle: fitted team ratings, model constants, the official fixture list and matchday dates, recent form, weekly odds snapshots, the live accuracy ledger, a reliability profile, per-club season stakes (title/top-4/top-6/relegation odds from a seeded Monte Carlo), and referee assignments when published. The PL Bookings Desk bakes these fitted ratings into its own build; the bundle is CORS-open for any other consumer too.
  • /clubs/index.json — index of all 20 clubs with title and relegation odds.
  • /clubs/<slug>.json — one club's finishing-position distribution, title/top-4/Europe/relegation odds, expected points and every remaining fixture's win/draw/loss probability (e.g. /clubs/arsenal.json).
Refreshed by the Monday recalibration. The PL Bookings Desk ingests these ratings at build time to weight its card markets by expected game state.
